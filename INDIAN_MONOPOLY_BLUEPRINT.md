# Indian Monopoly: "Kuber Vyuha" (The Great Indian Tycoon)
## Complete Game Design Blueprint & Production Specification

---

## 1. Game Concept & Theme

* **Official Game Name:** **KUBER: The Great Indian Property Empire** (or *Kuber Vyuha: The Indian Tycoon*)
* **Tagline:** *From Bazaars to Skyscrapers — Build Your Bharat Empire!*
* **Target Age Group:** 8 years to Adult (8+)
* **Number of Players:** 2 to 8 Players (Optimal: 4–6 Players)
* **Approximate Playing Time:** 60 to 120 minutes (Standard Game) / 45 minutes (Speed Dice / Short Rule Mode)
* **Core Objective:** Accumulate wealth by acquiring properties across India's iconic commercial hubs, developing residential floors and luxury high-rises, charging rent, managing cash flow, and driving competitors into financial insolvency (bankruptcy).
* **Indian Setting & Theme:** The game takes players on an epic economic journey across India’s rich geographical landscape — encompassing historic medieval markets (Chandni Chowk, Charminar), scenic coastal promenades (Puducherry, Goa, Kochi), royal heritage heritage belts (Jaipur, Varanasi), bustling industrial and planned metros (Pune, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh), thriving tech capitals (Bengaluru, Hyderabad HITEC City), and billionaire corridors (BKC, Lutyens' Delhi, Marine Drive).
* **Overall Visual Identity:** Regal Indian modernism. Rich warm jewel tones (Deep Indigo `#1A237E`, Royal Saffron `#E65100`, Emerald Green `#1B5E20`, Heritage Terracotta `#8D6E63`, and Imperial Crimson `#B71C1C`), combined with intricate *Jaali* lattice patterns, gold-foil embossing, and elegant bilingual typography (English with Devanagari aesthetic accents).
* **Design Philosophy:** Faithful mathematical balance honoring the classic property-trading board-game architecture while creating an authentic Indian socio-economic cultural experience — from *Challans* and *GST Cess* to *Diwali Bonuses* and *Panchayat Development Funds*.

### Mathematical Balancing Justification:
* **Base Rent:** Set between 2.5% to 12.5% of the land cost.
* **Monopoly Rule:** Owning all un-mortgaged properties of a color group doubles the base unimproved rent.
* **Exponential Construction Curve:** 1 house provides ~5x base rent, 2 houses ~15x, 3 houses ~45x, 4 houses ~65x, and a Hotel yields a lethal ~80x to 125x base rent. The 3rd house is the critical inflection point where return-on-investment (ROI) turns strongly cashflow positive.
* **Mortgage Value:** Exactly 50% of the purchase price. Un-mortgaging requires paying the mortgage amount + 10% statutory interest.

## 7. Special Board Spaces & Thematic Mechanics

### 1. Position 0: Aarambh (Shubh Aarambh / Start)
* **Theme:** Auspicious beginnings.
* **Mechanic:** Whenever a player passes or lands on Aarambh, they collect **₹2,000** as salary/dividend from the Bank.

### 2. Position 4: Aaykar (Income Tax)
* **Theme:** Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) annual filing.
* **Mechanic:** Player must pay a flat **₹2,000** (or an optional variant of 10% of total net worth) directly to the Bank.

### 3. Position 10: Police Chowki & Hawalat (Detention / Just Visiting)
* **Theme:** Local police precinct holding area.
* **Mechanic:**
  * **Just Visiting:** Passing or landing normally incurs zero penalty. Place token on the "Visitor Lobby" perimeter.
  * **In Detention:** Sent by landing on Space 30, rolling 3 doubles consecutively, or drawing a Detention card. Token is placed in the central cell.
  * **Escaping Detention:**
    1. Pay a bail fine of **₹500** before rolling on any of your next 3 turns.
    2. Play a *Zamanat Patra* (Get Out of Chowki Free) card.
    3. Roll doubles on your turn. If doubles are not rolled by Turn 3, the player **must pay ₹500** and moves forward by the dice sum.

### 4. Position 20: Vishram Sthal / Yatri Niwas (Free Parking)
* **Theme:** Public transit resthouse / scenic rest stop.
* **Mechanic:** A completely safe resting zone. No financial transaction takes place (or optional "House Rule" Jackpot where unpaid fines/taxes accumulate in the center).

### 5. Position 30: Nyayalay Saman / Giraftari (Go To Police Chowki)
* **Theme:** Judicial warrant / police apprehension.
* **Mechanic:** Move token immediately to Position 10 (In Detention). Cannot collect ₹2,000 salary even if passing Aarambh. Turn ends immediately.

### 6. Position 38: Vishesh Kar & GST Cess (Luxury / Wealth Tax)
* **Theme:** Luxury goods, gold bullion, and ultra-high-net-worth expenditure levy.
* **Mechanic:** Flat payment of **₹1,000** to the Bank.

### Bank Reserves & Total Supply (8-Player Edition)
* 40 × ₹5,000 notes (= ₹200,000)
* 50 × ₹2,000 notes (= ₹100,000)
* 80 × ₹1,000 notes (= ₹80,000)
* 100 × ₹500 notes (= ₹50,000)
* 100 × ₹100 notes (= ₹10,000)

### Banking Protocols:
* **The Banker:** Chosen at setup. Manages properties, auctions, houses/hotels, and cash reserves. Must keep personal funds strictly separated from Bank assets.
* **Mortgaging:** A player can mortgage any unimproved property to the Bank for 50% face value. Mortgaged properties generate zero rent. To un-mortgage, the player pays the Bank the mortgage amount plus 10% interest (rounded up).
* **Bankruptcy:** If a player owes more money than their total liquidated assets (cash + mortgage values + 50% value of sold buildings), they declare bankruptcy:
  * *Owed to an Opponent:* Surrender all cash, turn over mortgaged properties to that creditor (creditor must immediately pay 10% interest or un-mortgage).
  * *Owed to the Bank:* All properties are immediately auctioned un-mortgaged to the remaining players. The player is eliminated.

## 9. Houses & Hotels (Bhavans & Mahals)

* **Level 1–4: "Bhavan" (Residential Floor / Apartment Complex)**
  * *Physical Piece:* Emerald-Green stackable miniature colonial villas / modern apartment blocks.
  * *Rules:* Must build evenly across all properties in a complete color set (cannot have 2 Bhavans on one property and 0 on another in the same set).
* **Level 5: "Mahal" (Luxury High-Rise / Heritage Hotel)**
  * *Physical Piece:* Crimson-Red miniature luxury palace tower with gold domes.
  * *Rules:* Erected by trading in 4 Bhavans plus paying the specific property's Hotel construction price. Maximum 1 Mahal per property.
* **Component Scarcity (Building Shortage):**
  * The game contains strictly **32 Bhavans** and **12 Mahals**.
  * If the Bank has no Bhavans available, players cannot build until another player sells or upgrades to a Mahal. No bypassing allowed!

## 10. Complete Official Rules of Kuber

### 1. Setup
1. Unfold board on a flat surface. Shuffle **Kismat** and **Panchayat** card decks and place them face down on designated board spaces.
2. The Banker distributes exactly **₹15,000** to each player according to the denomination table.
3. Each player selects one metallic token and places it on **Aarambh (Position 0)**.

### 2. Turn Order & Movement
1. Each player rolls two 6-sided dice. Highest roll plays first; turns proceed clockwise.
2. On your turn, roll both dice and move your token clockwise that exact number of spaces.
3. **Doubles:** Rolling doubles awards an immediate extra turn. If you roll doubles **three consecutive times in one turn**, you are caught speeding: advance immediately to **Police Chowki (Detention)** without collecting ₹2,000 salary!

### 3. Buying & Auctioning Real Estate
1. Landing on an unowned Property, Transportation Hub, or Utility gives you the first right to buy it at listed Bank price.
2. **Decline to Buy / Public Auction:** If you decline to buy at list price, the Banker immediately conducts an open auction. Bidding begins at ₹100, and any player (including the one who declined) may bid. The highest bidder pays the Bank and receives the Title Deed.

### 4. Collecting Rent
1. Landing on an un-mortgaged property owned by an opponent requires paying the listed rent.
2. If the owner forgets to demand rent before the next player rolls the dice, the rent is forfeited.
3. **Full Color Set (Monopoly):** Doubles unimproved base rent even if no Bhavans are built.

### 5. Development & Construction
1. Once you own all properties of a single color group, you may construct **Bhavans** at any time between player turns.
2. Construction must follow the **Uniform Building Rule**: you cannot build a 2nd Bhavan on any property until all properties in that group have at least 1 Bhavan.
3. Upgrading to a **Mahal** requires trading in 4 Bhavans and paying the single Hotel fee.

### 6. Selling & Mortgages
1. Properties can be mortgaged to the Bank for 50% face value at any time, provided all buildings on that entire color group have been sold back to the Bank at half-price.
2. Mortgaged properties collect ₹0 rent, but other properties in that color group continue collecting standard rent.

### 7. Private Trading
1. Players may trade unbuilt properties, cash, or *Zamanat Patra* cards at any point on their turns for mutually agreed terms. No player may loan money to another player.

### 8. Winning the Game
The game ends when all opponents have gone bankrupt, leaving the final remaining tycoon as the undisputed **Kuber of India**!
